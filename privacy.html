PRIVACY POLICY

GİZLİLİK POLİTİKASI

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Who we are

Kayıtsal is a Turkish history documentary channel (YouTube: @kayitsal). We operate a small, private publishing tool ("the App") that posts our own short videos to our own Instagram and TikTok accounts.

What data the App uses

  • When the channel owner connects an Instagram or TikTok account, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (account ID, display name, avatar) from that platform.
  • The App uploads only videos and captions created by Kayıtsal.
  • The App does not collect, read or store data about any other users, viewers, followers or comments.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in our private hosting environment and are used only to publish our own content. They are never sold, shared or published.

Third parties

Content is sent only to the platform being published to (Instagram / Meta, TikTok), under that platform's own terms and privacy policy.

Deleting data

The channel owner can disconnect the App at any time from the Instagram or TikTok account settings; we then delete the stored tokens. Questions: contact us through the "About" page of the Kayıtsal YouTube channel.

Türkçe özet

Bu araç yalnız Kayıtsal'ın kendi videolarını kendi Instagram ve TikTok hesaplarına yükler. Başka hiçbir kullanıcının verisini toplamaz ya da saklamaz. Hesap bağlantısı için gereken erişim anahtarları şifreli olarak saklanır ve hiçbir şekilde paylaşılmaz. Bağlantı istendiğinde kaldırılır ve anahtarlar silinir.
